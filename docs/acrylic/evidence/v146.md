## Implementation

- `_page_select_tags()` is deterministic and debt-only. It calls v139's
  `locate.balance_sheet_page()` for `[balance sheet]`, and obtains table title blocks through the
  same bounded `_scope_zone()` walk consumed by `_table_scope()` for
  `[liquidity-risk / undiscounted]`. Consequently, a distant section heading separated from a
  table by prose does not label that table.
- The carrying tag is `[borrowings note: carrying]`: it uses only normalized words from the
  schema's existing `keywords` (including plural normalization), never a company list. A liquidity
  tag and a carrying tag are mutually exclusive; balance-sheet is the one independent tag, so a
  page has at most two tags.
- The debt-specific page-select system hint says to prefer carrying amounts in the borrowings note
  or balance sheet, and says contractual liquidity-risk / undiscounted pages are not the target
  unless no carrying candidate exists. Other schemas receive the prior untagged prompt.

### Prompt inserted for `debt_maturity`

> Pick the page whose table states carrying amounts of borrowings / interest-bearing liabilities
> (the borrowings note, or the balance sheet's interest-bearing lines). Pages tagged liquidity-risk
> / undiscounted list contractual cash flows and are not the target unless no other candidate holds
> the borrowings.
