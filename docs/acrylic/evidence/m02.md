## Coverage mechanisms and defaults

| Path | Mechanism | Default and isolation | Cost / conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EXTRACT_SCAN_ALL` | Tries the normal candidate pair, then every two-page document window; the window with the most non-null fields wins, and scanning stops only when every field is non-null. | Off unless explicitly `1`; never replaces an analyst/second-pass `fixed_pages` window. It is included in the extraction cache identity. The pre-existing `EXTRACT_TWO_PASS` precedence is unchanged. | Corpus page counts imply median **77** and p90 **110** logical model calls per report/section. It is an offline upper-bound/diagnostic tool, not a production default. |
| w197 + w198 `EXTRACT_SECOND_PASS` | For up to three missing required fields, tries the locator's bounded pages, then performs a zero-model whole-text synonym scan and reads at most three newly found pages. Every write still passes quote, value, label and scope guards. | Off unless explicitly enabled; bounded `fixed_pages` calls cannot be widened by SCAN_ALL. | At most six additional bounded calls. This remains the only production-shaped coverage experiment. |

Demo defaults are therefore unchanged and explicit: leave `EXTRACT_SECOND_PASS` and
`EXTRACT_SCAN_ALL` unset, and keep `EXTRACT_MERGE_RUNS=off`. `scripts/scan_all_compare.py` now also
accepts `LLM_PROVIDER=fixture` locally, without widening the production provider contract. Its
`income_statement --limit 1` smoke run completed both paths with provider/model `fixture`, **1 vs
71 logical attempts and zero external model calls**; fixture scores are intentionally meaningless.

## Teammate evaluation findings

- **Laya:** zero-shot default-threshold accuracy is **79.6%**, below the always-applicable naive
  baseline at **80.6%**. Train-calibrated threshold accuracy drops to **69.9%**. This is a negative
  zero-shot result, not a claim about an untested RLCD-fine-tuned model.
- **Docling:** the measured 163-page conversion took **1710 s** versus **6.3 s** for this parser,
  approximately **271× slower**. No accuracy comparison was completed, so the supported conclusion
  is speed-only; selective OCR remains the cheaper response to scanned pages.

## Remaining risk

SCAN_ALL's accuracy/cost comparison was not run against a real model because this lane is required
to make zero real inference calls. Its full-document winner rule can amplify a wrong dense table and
its cost is two orders of magnitude above the bounded path; it must remain opt-in and offline. Laya
and Docling conclusions are limited to the measured zero-shot and CPU/default-setting experiments.
