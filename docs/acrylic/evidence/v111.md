## The mechanism (all of it inside v103's `_table_scope` framework — no new walk, no new window)

`debt_maturity.json` gains two sub-lists under the existing `table_scope_words` key (v103's
`undiscounted`/`all_liabilities` untouched):

- `non_debt_subject` — `contract liabilities(/avtalsskulder)`, `timing of revenue`/
  `intäktsredovisning`, `customer advances`/`förskott från kunder`, `warranty`/`garanti`,
  `provisions`/`avsättningar`, `deferred revenue`, `upplupna` … — matched on the **title block only**
  (v103's `_scope_zone` window and `_SCOPE_TITLE_MAX` short-standalone-line rule, unchanged).
- `parent_company` — `Parent Company`, `Moderbolaget`, `Moderföretaget`, `Parent`.

`extract.py` adds, beside v103's helpers:

- `_debt_subject_words(schema)` — the rescue list of rule (a): the identity total field's own
  `synonyms` + `row_synonyms` plus the bare borrowing words (`interest-bearing`, `räntebärande`,
  `borrowings`, `lease liabilities`, `upplåning`, …). A debt word anywhere in the table's title block
  **or body rows** rescues the table — debt words win (the work order's made-up counter-example:
  a `Contract liabilities` title over `Interest-bearing liabilities` rows is NOT refused).
- `_section_heading(row, parent_words)` — `"parent" | "group" | None`: which entity a **short
  standalone row** names as a section heading. The entity word alone, with trailing unit/date
  furniture excepted (`Parent Company, MSEK 31 Dec 2025 31 Dec 2024` — Momentum p.117;
  `Moderbolaget 2025 2024` — Svedbergs p.132): everything from the first digit-bearing token on is
  furniture, a trailing unit word may follow. A row naming **both** entities is v052's paired-column
  header (`Koncernen … Moderbolaget …`, MedCap p.101's `Förfallotidpunkt för upplåning Koncernen
  Moderbolaget`) — one table's two column groups, never a section heading, keeps its own read. A prose
  line that merely mentions the entity (`Moderbolaget har inga räntebärande skulder`) never survives
  the equality — v103's short-standalone-line rule tightened to the entity word itself.
- `_nearest_entity_section(rows, i, parent_words)` — the first parent/group heading an upward walk
  (25 rows, v103's window) meets above the candidate row decides: a table under a Group heading is the
  Group's whatever sits further up, and a Parent Company section holds **until** a
  Group/Koncernen/Consolidated heading re-opens the Group's own tables — Momentum p.117's `Group, MSEK`
  below Note 23's Parent block is exactly that return.

Two verdicts, both refused under **both** bases (checked before v103's basis branching, after the
carrying-column exemption — a table that prints a carrying column stays readable, v076/v085):
`non_debt` (title block hits a non-debt word, no debt word in title or body) and `parent`
(nearest entity section is "parent"). `_REFUSED_SCOPES` = v103's two + these two; every gate that
v103 added now refuses all four, and the two gates this batch adds:

1. the **statement-spread fills** in `extract()` (both the model-null page fill and the
   "not printed on the statement" refill) — the mechanism that wrote Volati's 87 over the model's 192
   and Momentum's 2/2/0 out of the parent table; refusing at the fill means the model's own answer
   stands. This gate also carries v103's refusals at fill time (they were previously caught post-hoc
   by the citation gate) — Smartcraft's replay warnings drop 5→2 for exactly that reason: the
   undiscounted-table fill is now declined before the value is ever written (same final values,
   cleaner provenance; the only replay-visible delta is the warning count).
2. nothing else new: `_between_rows`, `_finer_split_rows`, `_date_bucket_derive`,
   `_fill_bucket_columns`, `_subtotal_pair_fill` (v110) and the model-citation gate all already route
   through `_table_scope` and now receive the two new verdicts via `_REFUSED_SCOPES`.
   `_prior_year_fill` (v091) reads only a field the same mechanisms already gated the first read of —
   its source row passed the same gates on the way in. v109 (`_buckets_by_year_fill`) has **not**
   landed on `acrylic` at delivery (checked by fetch); whoever merges it must thread its entry through
