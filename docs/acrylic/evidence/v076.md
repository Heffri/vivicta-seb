## The shape, and why v069 left it

Ework p.70's liquidity-risk table prints **eight** columns,
`Due / < 1 month / 1-3 months / 3-12 months / 1-5 years / > 5 years / Total undiscounted value / Carrying amount`:
two total-shaped columns where the mechanism had one slot. v069 left it honestly declined ("adding a second total
slot risks summing both trailing columns into total_debt on some other company's table where they are not equal").
That risk is real — with future interest, the undiscounted total **exceeds** the carrying total — and it is what
dictates the design: the second slot is not another total, it is a recognised-and-counted column that is **never
assigned**, and the carrying column is the one that becomes `total_debt` (v028's own stated basis).

## The design (schema names both word groups; extract maps them to slots)

`backend/schemas/debt_maturity.json`:

- `total_debt` gets `header_synonyms`: `["carrying amount", "carrying value", "redovisat värde", "bokfört värde"]`.
  Carrying wording is the total column's own header — the natural home for it. (The work order's three examples
  plus `carrying value`, the other standard IFRS column header for the same thing.)
- New top-level `ignore_header_synonyms`: `["total undiscounted", "undiscounted cash flows", "odiskonterade
  kassaflöden", "total contractual"]` — an explicit structure expressing "this column exists, count it, assign
  nothing", exactly the work order's "别硬塞进桶键".

`backend/pipeline/extract.py`, inside the existing span-based hit machinery (v069's `_drop_nested_hits` discipline):

1. `_bucket_synonym_hits(text, bucket_sfs, total_sf=None, ignore_syns=None)`: carrying words hit → span tagged
   `total:carrying`; ignore words → span tagged `_ignore`; same dash-normalised literal scan as every
   header_synonym. Nothing else changes for bucket fields.
2. `_bucket_header(...)`: a carrying/ignore hit survives **only on a row that also names at least one bucket**
   (the real header line). Ework's own p.70 carries, inside the same 25-row header window, the note title
   "Maturity structure financial liabilities – undiscounted cash flows" and the prose sentence "…reflected in the
   carrying amount of…" — v069's bare-`due` lesson one more time, fenced the same way (the same guard is what
   keeps Acast's and Nederman's non-bucket "Carrying amount" headers reading exactly as before). On a line that
   does name buckets: carrying present → the line's bare Total word is demoted to `_ignore` (the carrying column
   IS the total; a competing total-shaped column must not be summed into it); no carrying on the line → the
   `_ignore` tags are dropped and the line's own bare word keeps today's reading (a header with only "Total
   undiscounted value" still reads that column as the total slot — the work order's undiscounted-only fallback).
   The ≥2-distinct-bucket gate counts bucket stems only, so carrying/ignore words never qualify a page.
3. `_bucket_assign` skips `_ignore` — never assigned, and (load-bearing) never fed to `over`: an undiscounted
   total above the carrying total is ordinary future interest, never grounds to reject the row. The column-count
   safety valve needs `_ignore` columns **in** the count — Ework's row must show 8 hits for 8 amounts
   (命中数(含忽略)= 行金额数, as the work order has it).
4. `_bucket_row_prior_year` gains a lone-year fallback (below).

## Three real-page catches between the design and the green (each fenced at the source)

The isolated header+row read green immediately; the full real p.70 did not, three times — recorded in order:

1. **A prose `total` word padding the count.** First end-to-end run:
   `total_debt: column reading of 'Total 91,284 1,625,763 998,715 127,860 22,169 794 2,866,584 2,866,462 2024'
   rejected -- due_within_1_year 2774507, due_after_5_years 2866584 exceed its own total 93308; model's own values
   kept` — the prose row two tables above ("…36 (32)% of the **total** balance for accounts…") had contributed a
   bare-total hit since v060, harmless while counts never matched; the carrying slot made 9 hits meet that Total
   row's 9 (glue-`2024`-inflated) amounts, shifting every column by one. The `over` valve caught it — that is what
   it is for — and the fence is: when the window carries a carrying column, bare-total words are demoted to
   `_ignore` **on bucket-naming lines** and dropped from prose lines entirely (they are not columns).
2. **The 2024 table's own Total row reading under the 2025 header.** With counts finally level, the prior-year
   Total row (`Total 103,815 … 3,300,679`, 8 amounts = the 8-key header four rows up) read clean through every
   valve — the v066 `_bucket_row_prior_year` walk uses `_year_run`, which needs a **run** of ≥2 years, and Ework
   stacks its two tables printing **one** year label each, glued by `_page_rows` as lone trailing tokens
   ("… Carrying amount 2025", "… 2,866,462 2024"). Same nearest-window walk, lone year tokens: the nearest year
   named above the row answers the valve's question alone. The row is now rejected with
   `names fiscal year 2024, not 2025; not this year's bucket row`.
3. The note-title and prose rows themselves (fence 0, in place from the start — without it every candidate reads
   9–10 hits against 8 amounts and the lane never leaves red).

After all three: exactly one candidate survives on p.70 — the debt row — and the fill is the one the table prints.

## The check stays `missing`, honestly (premise overturn — reported, not forced)

The work order expected 恒等式在 ±2 内通过. The arithmetic does close — 156,409 + 0 + 0 vs 156,410, off by 1 — but
`maturity_sums_to_total` reports `missing: due_1_to_5_years`, not passed. Reason, entirely in out-of-territory
machinery: the buckets here are null because their cells print dashes (v036's own convention — a dash column is
unprinted, not a fabricated zero; the same convention v069 enshrined in `eval/labels.csv`'s notes for exactly these
cells), and the v041/v058 null-bucket guard in `_check` refuses to zero-fill a null bucket whose synonym is
visible in the operands' own table — which it is: "1-5 years" sits in the table's own header row, inside the
span. Making the check pass would require either writing 0 for dash cells (contradicts v036/v069 and the labels)
or touching `_check` (out of territory). Reported per reporting.md's premise-overturn rule: the **values** are the
deliverable and they are right; the check's verdict is the machinery's honest "the table does print these buckets,
so a null is unproven". Note the asymmetry that makes the missing-fields loop settle on `due_1_to_5_years` first:
`> 5 years` (the symbol form) is a `_BUCKET_BOUNDARY`/Swedish-synonym word only, so the guard's plain-synonym scan
does not see it in the header — `due_1_to_5_years`' "1-5 years" it does.
