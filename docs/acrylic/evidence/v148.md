  not read even on the right page — sol shares the family's partial-read shape.

  [93,94] and answered p.93's 548320) and read all three fields ✓✓✓ from p.92. This is a real

  publishing it changes nothing; it only shows the selector's coin landed differently.
- Page-select overall: sol selected at least one label page for 15/22 (bts and sdiptech are the
  two where that flip produced a label-right read terra's fresh runs missed; raysearch is the
  mirror: sol's [67,68] *lost* the total terra's [72,73] read right, answering 395@67 — wrong

## The hard companies — sol also misreads; these are 口径/页面形状, not model capacity

1. **Scope conventions (labels take a narrower row than the note's own total):** momentum (sol
   again 622 incl. the lease row, label 395), dustin (sol again 3055 incl. finance leases, label
   2538). Sol reproduces terra's convention misses byte-for-byte — a schema/prompt ruling, not a
   model-capacity gap.
2. **Wrong-year/period columns:** better_collective — stored has the FY2024 comparative 259946;
   sol produced a *third* value 271922@162. Two models, three answers, zero right: the year
   disambiguation on that table needs a mechanism, not a stronger model.
3. **Unit scale:** storytel's 550000 reproduces under sol. Same for the rounding-tier miss on
   raysearch (395 vs 394.9).
4. **Decline-on-right-page (页面形状):** oresund ([47,48] selected, all null — sol repeats terra's
   v124 behavior exactly), ovzon, flerie, elanders's and ratos's prose-only figures — where terra
   honestly declined, sol *invents* (elanders 8864@130, ratos 7907@138): eval-neutral against a
   null store but publish-hostile (wrong non-null where the stored answer is honestly null).
5. **ovzon's guard collision (new instance of the ework family):** sol *did* answer T 423 and
   W 40 — the label values — but both were **refused by the wrong-table guard** ("the Parent
   Company section holds this table"): sol read the right numbers out of a Parent-Company-headed
   section. The stored null stands; the guard vs model-answer collision on this page is recorded
   for whoever owns the semantics.
6. **net_insight** — sol's pass-1 picked the wrong-scope sibling page [101,102] again, yet every
   field source landed on the labelled p.91 table and the answers are byte-identical to stored,
   including the ✗ >5y=0 (label wants the field absent). The sibling-table trap did not bite this
   time; the label's own >5y dispute remains the only miss, under both models.
