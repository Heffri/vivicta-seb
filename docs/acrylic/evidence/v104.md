## The page is torn in two ways (`data/kb/svedbergs_2025/pages.jsonl` p.132)

1. **Side-by-side glue**: the maturity table and the financing-changes note print in two columns,
   and pymupdf glues the note's lines onto the maturity rows — every data row and the Summa row:

   ```
   Mellan 2 år och 5 år 11 896 19 122 — — Förändringar leasingskuld 360 125 50 547 410 672 — — —
   Summa 496 405 886 478 432 824 609 480 Per 31 december 2025 865 828 101 399 967 226 412 211 20 613 432 824
   ```

   `_row_amounts` anchors on the row's **last** alpha token — inside the glued note — so on the
   baseline every row read returns the OTHER table's figures or none: `Mer än 5 år` read
   `[-46145, -13437, -59583, -45004, -2374, -47378]` (the cash-flow table's valuations!), the
   Summa row read `[2025, 865828…, 20613…]`. Taking the fiscal-year column of those numbers is
   exactly the wrong-column trap the work order flagged.
2. **Em-dash nils**: the parent-company pair prints as em dashes (U+2014) — `"11 896 19 122 — —"`
   — and `_DASHES` normalises en dash/minus/nbsp only.

## Mechanism (`backend/pipeline/extract.py`, additions only; schema untouched — v088 rejection 3 and v097's H2 replay stand: these wordings stay out of the synonym lists)

- **`_bucket_span`**, the interval-geometry parser (used by `_finer_split_rows` only): the
  between/mellan branch now takes **a unit per operand** — `mellan N unit och M unit`, mixed units
  included (`Mellan 3 månader och 1 år` → [3,12], `Mellan 1 år och 2 år` → [12,24],
  `Mellan 2 år och 5 år` → [24,60], `mellan 18 månader och 2 år` → [18,24]); the unit-less form
  (`between 1 and 2 years`, `mellan 1 och 5 år`) is unchanged; reversed bounds (`mellan 5 år och
  2 år`) → None. `högst` joins the bounded-below forms (`högst 2 år` → [0,24]). Forms the baseline
  already read and this lane exercises on the real page: `N månader eller mindre` → [0,N],
  `mer än / över / efter N år` → [12N,∞), `inom N unit` → [0,12N]. Still geometry, still
  deliberately blind to bare `y/yr` and day wording.
- **`_finer_split_rows`**, the year-column call for the walk's rows (the in-territory adjustment):
  the ordinary `_row_amounts(row, ncols)` is tried first — byte-identical on clean pages, which is
  why 201 of 203 replay companies are same — and a row it cannot line up falls to the
  **label-anchored read**: with the label known (`_row_label`), this table's own columns are what
  prints between the label and the next label word — amounts left to right, a lone dash (em dash
  included, `_TORN_DASHES`) as the printed nil, the first lettered token ending the row, anything
  else unparseable ending it too. Two guards keep it honest: space-groups degroup **one group per
  match** (`_TORN_GROUP` — `_SPACE_GROUPS`' greedy run fuses the row's own adjacent 3-digit cells
  into one 24-digit number), and only the year header's own fiscal-year column is ever used; a row
  that cannot produce exactly `ncols` cells breaks the walk — **取不到就放弃,不猜** (the ruling's
  wording). The total row goes through the same reader.

## Byggmästare — 未交付缺口 (ruling 2026-09-18: shape recorded, another lane's work)

The work order's premise — a lease table printed as coarse rows (`år 1 / år 2–5 / efter år 5`) on
the total's own page — does not hold on the real pages:

- The real rows are **per-year** (`data/kb/byggmastare_a_j_ahlstrom_h_2025/pages.jsonl` p.54,
  verbatim lines): `Amotering år 1` 962/853, `Amotering år 2` 1054/1 002, `Amotering år 3`
  857/1 082, `Amotering år 4` -/868 (two columns 2025/2024; the page text prints `Amotering`,
  v097's "Amortering" transcription is the model quote's rendering). The target value is
  confirmed from the page: **1,911 = 1,054 + 857 + 0** (år 2+3+4, 2025 column).
- Three structural blockers, all outside this lane's territory (`_bucket_span` forms + the
  year-column call only):
  1. **Cross-page table**: the header `Löptidsanalys leaseskulder 2025 2024` is the LAST line of
     p.53 and the per-year rows are on p.54, while the stored total anchor
     `Redovisade leasingskulder 31 december 2 873 3 806` (the classification table's own total) is
     on p.53 with only non-interval rows above it — the walk deliberately never leaves the total's
     page (v096 scope), so it can never see the rows.
  2. **Label swallows the year number**: `_row_label("Amotering år 1 962 853")` = `"Amotering år"`
     — the `1` reads as the start of a space-grouped `1 962` — so even on p.54 `_bucket_span`
     would receive a number-less label. Fixing needs a `_row_label`/`år N` disambiguation decision.
  3. **No year header on p.54** (`_year_column` None): the `2025 2024` header sits one page up,
     below the total on p.53.
