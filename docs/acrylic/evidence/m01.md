6. **Fetch storage and progress:** kept v193 atomic PDF/index publication, corrupt-cache cleanup and v194 `jobs.step()` events around every source, while adding URL quoting, issuer validation and structured `FetchAttempts` reasons.
7. **Registration and OCR:** checksum rejection, same-file fast return and publish-after-parse were combined with v191 bounded/full OCR and `ocr_pages_needed`; w204's packaged tessdata path was retained together with UTF-8 child-process output.
8. **API client and docs:** `ApiError` carries `code`, `tried`, `attempts` and `ocrPagesNeeded`; discover documentation retains `source`, `skipped_web_search`, `force_web` and `job_id`, while fetch documents both w202's 409 parent-report guard and the new failure taxonomy.
9. **Library/map surface:** strict digest-based `pdf_available` remains the PDF truth and is combined with w202 `reported_members`, sector metadata and parent-report opening.
