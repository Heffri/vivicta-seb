## Why v110's join did not fire — two gates, not one

The work order asked for the first failing gate; the honest answer is that **two gates block
Enea, in series** — fixing either alone still ends in silence.

**Gate 1 — the title gate (first for the note's own block).** `_find_pair` starts only from a
heading-like row carrying a debt word of `total_debt`'s synonyms + row_synonyms. Page 76 has
exactly three such rows, and none heads the liabilities table:

| row | why it cannot start the walk |
|---|---|
| `1) Enea has a bank loan with a term of 3 years. ...` | footnote prose; its walk reaches the maturity analysis's own subject row as a fake "section A" and dies at the sumA gate (no bare-Total row within 14 rows below) — no false fire, but never the real block |
| `Non-current and current interest-bearing liabilities` | the maturity analysis's subject row; rows below it print figures within the title window, and it has no section subtotals at all |
| `Non-interest bearing liabilities` | section C of the maturity analysis; same — figures below, no pair |

The note's own subject line — `Financial liabilities 2025 2024 2025 2024`, heading-like (years
only) and directly above section A — carries **no** schema debt word: the lists name totals
("total borrowings", "total interest-bearing liabilities", …) and instrument rows ("bank loans",
"liabilities to credit institutions", …), never the IFRS note-family wording. The walk never
starts.

**Gate 2 — `_vocab`'s dash table.** Even with a title, section A does not exist: the heading
prints the **non-breaking hyphen U+2011** (`Non‑current liabilities, interest‑bearing`), and
`_DASHES` folds U+2013 and U+2212 but not U+2011. Untranslated, the ASCII tail "current" inside
"non‑current" still matches `_CURRENT_WORDS` — the A-rules-first ordering (v110's own comment:
"'non-current' contains 'current', so A rules first") holds only while the hyphen normalizes, so
the NON-current heading is filed under **B** and no A heading exists anywhere in the block.

Everything downstream was verified passable on the real text once both gates open: both Summas
resolve the same year header `(column 0, 4 columns)` (the Group | Parent duplicate-year pair,
disambiguated by the "Group Parent Company" header above), each section closes trivially (one
detail row = its Summa in all four columns), the sumA→sumB span is a contiguous page substring
under `quote_on_page`, the tail gate reads only the first non-heading row after sumB
("Accounts payable …" — not a total row; the grand total 334,759 sits 10 rows down, outside the
window, and is not total_debt-shaped anyway), and the wrong-table guard (`_table_scope` on the
sumB row) returns "unknown" — no refused scope.

## The fix (`_subtotal_pair_fill` only, 19 lines incl. comments)

1. **Title gate**: `title_words = debt_words + ["financial liabilities", "finansiella skulder"]`
   — the note-family subject wording, used **only** by `_find_pair`'s title-row check
   (`_prose_total` keeps the schema's own vocabulary, so no widened word can fabricate a prose
   corroboration).
2. **`_vocab`**: `.replace("‑", "-")` after the `_DASHES` translation — the non-breaking
   hyphen is a print variant of the dash the table already folds; patched inside the walk, not in
   the shared `_DASHES`, which feeds every other gate in the file.

Greppability of every new word/shape (work-order rule), counted over `data/kb` + all 16 shared
seed KBs:

- `"financial liabilities"` (case-insensitive): **172 data/kb stems** (4c, aak, acast, acroud,
  addnode, addtech, …), present in every seed KB (e.g. seed-fin: avarda_bank, catella, enity,
  flat_capital, hoist_finance, intrum; seed-rex: ambea, attendo, bhg, humble …).
- `"finansiella skulder"`: **30 data/kb stems** (academedia, alligo, beijer_alma, bravida_holding,
  camurus, clas_ohlson, …) + 7 in seed-fin (flerie, linc, oresund, svolder, tangen, traction …),
  and NOTE's own report (seed-rex `note_2025`) prints it.
- **U+2011 in a current/non-current heading-like row with a debt-family word**: only
  `enea_2025` p.76 (`Non‑current liabilities, interest‑bearing`) among debt_maturity-replayable
  stems — plus `bure_equity_2025`'s bare `Non‑current liabilities` headings, and Bure has **no
  stored debt_maturity extraction** (not replayable, not reachable). The corpus otherwise prints
  U+2011 only in prose/CSR text.

Why this is not v041/v053's computed-not-read danger: the adopted 305,555 is the sum of two
**printed, individually closure-proven** subtotal rows — section structure, per-column closure,
title-scoped subject, tail-guarded, wrong-table-guarded. Nothing is derived from arbitrary rows;
the inference is exactly v110's, on its second real instance.
