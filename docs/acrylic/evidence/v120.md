## Method — the v043/v045 offline harness, A and B symmetrical

Per company: `kb._pages(stem)` from `data/kb` → `locate.candidate_pages(texts, schema)` **once** (A and B
share the candidate list; the delta touches only description text, not keywords/synonyms — verified) →

`kb.save_extraction` never fires. `data/kb` and `data/reports` are byte-untouched at delivery
(`git status` shows only the new evidence directory). A = the repo's own `debt_maturity.json` loaded as a
dict; B = the same dict with the sentences below appended **in memory** — the repo schema file was never
edited during the runs, so what would be committed is byte-what-was-tested (the strings live verbatim in
this file and in the lane's `b_delta.json`). A few-shot pool, page texts, candidates: identical for both
versions; the only delta is the five sentences. Route rule: each `(stem, version)` response is written to
[`v120/`](v120/) as `<stem>.{A,B}.debt_maturity.json` before the next call and never re-sent (LESSONS 37).

## The B wording (candidate; ≤5 sentences, no company names)

Four sentences appended to the schema `description`:

> Take every figure from the borrowings note or the balance sheet's own interest-bearing rows — never from
> the statement of cash flows, whose borrowings lines are the year's movements (repayments and new
> borrowings), not the period-end balance. Read the table's year header before reading any number: the
> column headed with the later year is the current fiscal year (a year ending March 2026 is reported in the
> 2026 column) and the earlier year is the prior year — never take the prior-year column, and figures in
> parentheses after a prose sentence are the prior year's. When only the liquidity-risk note's undiscounted
> contractual cash-flow table lies on the pages shown, still do not read from it: decline the field unless
> the borrowings note itself prints the figure — its totals, its bucket rows, or a prose sentence such as
> 'the part that falls due within 1 year amounts to X' all count as printed. When a note splits its
> borrowings only into current and non-current subtotals, report each subtotal through its own field: the
> current subtotal is due_within_1_year, and the non-current subtotal is due_1_to_5_years when the note's
> own text bounds it within five years.

One sentence appended to `total_debt.description`:

> When no printed row combines the current and non-current subtotals, total_debt is null — never add the
> subtotals yourself.

Class mapping: sentence 1 = cash-flow-statement figures (dynavox's v116 shape); sentence 2 = the
prior-year column (bergman & beving's stored miss is exactly the comparative column — `2,390 2,062`, stored
2,062 — and its year ends March 2026, the trap the later-year rule handles order-independently); sentence
3 = the undiscounted table (inwido/meren label shapes, whose notes say the undiscounted table was *not*
used) plus the prose-sentence acceptance (cellavision's buckets live in a prose sentence); sentence 4 +
the total sentence = the current/non-current-subtotals shape (smartcraft/sdiptech/bioinvent label shapes).
Since v045, pass-1 embeds the schema description too — the wording therefore also acts on page selection,
which is where several of the effects below come from.

## What the five sentences actually did

1. **Where the note is inside the candidate window, the wording worked — all four gains are in-window
   companies, and three of them are verbatim the targeted classes.** Bergman & beving's prior-year-column
   miss (the stored 2,062 is the 31 Mar 2025 comparative) did not reproduce under B: sentence 2's
   later-year rule took the current column of `"Total current liabilities 424 476"`. Sdiptech's current
   subtotal (sentence 4's exact case: non-current `Total 4,049` + current `Total 446`, no combined row)
   came through as w1y 446. Xano's >5y and xvivo's BS-lease w1y are plain "the printed row now gets
   answered" gains on rows A left null.
2. **The decline-encouragement overshoots — dynavox, the one ✓→✗.** A's pass-1 exercised the −1 companion
   override (`[133,134]`) and read the true borrowings note on p134 (`Interest-bearing loans 67.5 828.8 -
   896.2`: buckets sum exactly to 896.2, the label value, check-passing, quote-verified). B's pass-1 kept
   `[134,135]`, and B's pass-2 then declined **all four** fields even though the note's page was inside
   its own window — p135, the cash-flow statement sentence 1 warns about, sits right next to it, and the
   wording's caution won over the clause that licenses reading a printed borrowings row. A correct,
   verified answer became an honest null. That is the sentence doing what it says, at a price the verdict
   rule does not accept.
3. **Description text reaches pass-1 and can steer onto a wrong-scope table that passes the identity.**
   Cellavision: A picked [77,78] (credit-institutions pages) and declined everything; B jumped to [84,85]
   and answered **all four fields at confidence 1.0** from p84's `Maturity analysis of lease liabilities`
   (`Total 23,300`, buckets 12,213+8,109+2,978 — internally exact, so `maturity_sums_to_total` holds) —
   the label's financing-reconciliation total is 25,651. The v043 "confidently wrong, checks-passing"
   shape, newly reachable through prompt-steered page selection. Note for whoever owns the scope guard:
   `"Maturity analysis of lease liabilities"` is not in `table_scope_words`' refuse vocabulary — a
   one-line vocabulary candidate, at the usual replay-attested bar.
4. **The dominant wall is reachability, not wording.** 14 of the 20 companies have at least one label page
   outside `locate.candidate_pages`' list; for **9 label pages no prompt can reach the row at all**
   (cellavision 92, CTT 52, meren 73, byggmax 99, viaplay 113, inwido 166, lime 91, oresund 47 — whose
   candidate list is *empty* — and hexatronic 114), and 5 more are adjacent-only (dynavox 133, ovzon 61,
   tangen 62, smartcraft 58, xvivo 92 — reachable only when pass-1 names the −1 companion, which A did
   once and B's steer flipped away). The stored-✗ population this round drew from is, at the prompt layer,
   mostly a **locator-✗** population: tangen/viaplay/inwido/cellavision repeat their stored wrong shapes
   verbatim because the window leaves nothing else to read. Any prompt A/B on this corpus is testing the
   wording only on the ~8 companies whose notes are actually in reach; this one happened to move 4 fields
   up and 1 down there.
5. No regression anywhere traces to sentences 1–3's remaining clauses or the total sentence: the
   parenthetical-prose clause and the undiscounted decline never fired on a printed carrying row, and
   smartcraft's lease roll-forward stayed honestly null in both versions.

## For the group (what a future lane should pick up)

1. **Reachability is the binding constraint, and it is cheap to measure.** `candidate_pages` runs offline
   in milliseconds per stem; annotating each label company with "label page ∈ candidates / adjacent-only /
   unreachable" before seeding a hardening round would stop rounds from re-rolling companies no prompt or
   mechanism can move, and would make prompt A/Bs honest about their effective n (here ~8 of 20). The
   locator-side lead: oresund's *empty* candidate list and the 5 adjacent-only pages are exactly where a
   pass-1 companion-±1 default (or a note-heading-based candidate expansion) pays.
2. **If decline-encouragement wording is tried again, pair it with the printed-row license up front.**
   Dynavox's B decline happened with the correct row in-window, next to the exact page the warning was
   about; the sentence order ("never X … decline unless Y") let the prohibition win. One reversal test:
   license before prohibition ("read the borrowings note's own rows even when a cash-flow page sits
   beside them; never read the cash-flow rows themselves").
3. **`"Maturity analysis of lease liabilities"` → `table_scope_words`** is a one-line refuse-vocabulary
   candidate with a page-verified wrong-scope read behind it (cellavision B, 23,300 vs 25,651 at
   confidence 1.0 through a passing identity check) — same family as v103's targets, new marker.
4. **Prompt A/Bs on this schema should score page marks, not only values** — B moved pages +4 with zero
   page regressions; a value-only ledger would have under-reported it. (The same table with page marks
   counted as ✓s: 32 → 39.)
