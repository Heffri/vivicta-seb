## The wrong-scope read this names (v115's cellavision pass1, reproduced)

CellaVision Note B8 (p.84) prints its lease maturity table under `Maturity analysis of lease
liabilities Group Group` — `Total 23,300 27,522` summing `- Within one year 12,213`, `- Later than
one but within five years 8,109`, `- Later than within five years 2,978`. v115's pass1 read exactly
those four rows at confidence **1.0 each, check passed** (the identity endorses the table's own
arithmetic: 12,213+8,109+2,978 = 23,300), every raw label unknown to the vocabulary and kept by
`identity_all_columns`. The labels say **25,651 / 13,680 / 8,768 / 3,203** off p.92's Note C9
(financing reconciliation: 3,821 liabilities to credit institutions + 21,830 lease liability;
the prose names the three buckets) — leases are only part of the financing total, so the lease
note's table is the wrong scope, not the wrong company.

Why every earlier guard passes it: the title carries the debt word `lease liabilities`, so the
v111 non-debt rule's rescue wins *by design* (debt words win); there is no carrying column, no
parent section; and v103's `undiscounted` list held only RVRC's form, `maturity analysis
regarding` — `of lease liabilities` matched nothing, and `_table_scope` returned **`unknown` at
all four cited rows** (observed on the real p.84 rows before the edit). The internal arithmetic
then endorses the read — exactly v115's "self-consistent wrong-scope siblings" family.

The work order's Swedish forms — `Förfalloanalys av leasingskulder`, `leasingskuldernas

The 25 pages of the first form are lease tables (CellaVision p.84, Scandic p.138 `…according to
IFRS 16`, SSAB p.142 `…, undiscounted amounts`, Duni p.176 `…, undiscounted…`), a table-of-contents
line (Investor p.136) and prose pointers (`A maturity analysis of lease liabilities is presented in
Note …`); none is any company's debt_maturity label page. The one stem whose label cites a page
carrying the phrase is **BioInvent p.66** — and there the hit is the pointer line `For maturity
analysis of lease liabilities, see Note 21 …`, which sits *below* the labelled rows (`Long term
1,157`, `Short term 7,370`, `… 8,527` — BioInvent's only debt is leases, the label reads them) and
can never enter a title block: the note reference in it is a digit, and `_scope_zone`'s digit break
stops the walk above such lines (the corpus's other pointer lines — `see Note 2`, `see note 22`,
`Note 27` — share that furniture). So the new words only ever fire on real title rows like
CellaVision's own digit-free one; the committed block pins BioInvent's labelled rows staying
readable, Rusta's dash-form title (`Maturity analysis – lease liabilities …`, the lease-only
issuer's own table, v096's label-correct read) staying readable, and the pointer line's
non-membership.

Placement note: `non_debt_subject` cannot carry these words — their titles contain a debt word and
the v111 rescue would always win. `undiscounted` is the family both co-markers live in; under the
carrying basis the verdict is `undiscounted` (refused), under the undiscounted basis the marker
alone never refuses (v089's semantics — RVRC refuses there only through its payables rows), so the
verdict falls through to `unknown` and the read stays open there. Both behaviours asserted in the
committed block.
