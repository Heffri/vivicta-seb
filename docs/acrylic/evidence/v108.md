## For the group (what a future lane should pick up)

1. **The mis-scope family has a carrying-table edition.** v103 refuses
   undiscounted-titled and all-liabilities tables; this round adds two refusals it
   cannot express: Volati's fill came from the *contract-liabilities* timing note and
   Momentum's from the *Parent Company* lease table. A scope guard keyed on
   entity/note identity ("a Group total must not take buckets from a Parent-only
   table"; "a borrowings total must not take buckets from another note's timing
   table") is the next general rule; v095's debt-row-first ordering and v103's
   anatomy walk are the patterns to extend.
2. **Per-calendar-year maturity analyses** (BTS `2026 … 2030 Total`) want their own
   read path: the values are single printed rows (not even sums, except the 1–5y
   span), the model sums them correctly, and only the joined-label quote match fails —
   today they land at 0.25 forever. `_bucket_span` deliberately does not parse bare
   years; a year-sequence reader (anchored like `_finer_split_rows`, gated by the same
   identity) is the shape, with v096's "never write a bucket the table does not print"
   rule kept (BTS's `>5y` stays honestly null).
3. **Nobia's dash zeros** are the third instance of the printed-nil-cap family
   (v090 Linc, v097 Rejlers, now with the table printing the dashes in the buckets'
   own rows): the model answers 0 correctly, the zero-quote gate caps at 0.50, and
   full confidence is structurally unreachable until the dash-to-0 conversion can
   stamp `printed_nil` on a model answer, not only on a repair's.
4. **Arctic Paper's two-table carrying note** (short-term / long-term totals with a
   repayment-date column each) is a clean current/non-current read that nevertheless
   leaves `total_debt` null because no row prints the combined 251,209 — the
   short+long completion is the same arithmetic the computed-not-read guard refuses to
   invent; whether that specific sum (the note's own two printed totals) may be
   joined is an extract-layer ruling worth one line in the schema description.
