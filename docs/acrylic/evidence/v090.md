## The schema commit and its A/B evidence (`7903b27`)

Word lists are untouched — the page scans found no general synonym worth adding (see the rejected
candidates below). The change is the **ruling**, in the schema `description` and `total_debt`
`description`, both page-verified first:

> for a deposit-taking bank, borrowings = the report's own "total interest-bearing liabilities" row
> when printed (such totals include customer deposits); otherwise customer deposits are the bank's
> operating funding, not borrowings, and no single borrowed-money sub-row may stand in for the total —
> leave the fields null rather than summing rows; never read the liquidity note's undiscounted table.

- **Hoist (win, live):** pass1 (old text) — two-pass picked the undiscounted note [126,127]; the model
  still reached p.21 via the widen and returned 52,680@**0.95**. pass2 (new text) — two-pass picked
  **[21,22] directly** and returned 52,680@**1.0**. Same number, verified provenance, no widen.
- **Morrow (win, live):** pass1 read 265@0.9 — the subordinated-loans row standing in for a total that
  does not exist (v041 flagged exactly this risk). pass2 under the new text: **null**, correctly.
- **Intrum (no harm, live):** byte-identical full-confidence reading both passes.
- **Catella (not fixed, honestly recorded):** pass1 produced an unverified 1,324@0.25 (the balance-sheet
  total, right magnitude, no provenance — `parse.py`'s two-column scramble); pass2 produced
  -1,474@**1.0** — a quote-verified read of the *undiscounted, negative-signed* liquidity row. One
  observation each way at a model with documented call-to-call variance (v041): the change cannot be
  blamed for the negative, but it demonstrably did not rescue Catella either. Its defect is the parser
  scramble, out of this lane's territory.

### Word additions built and rejected before spending anything

- **Bank row words as synonyms** (`subordinated debt/loans/liabilities`, `debt securities issued`,
  `certificates and bonds`): rejected on v041's own reasoning, now with this round's pages — each is a
  single *component* row; Morrow pass1 shows exactly what happens when one stands in for a total.
- **Ratos's header words** (`within 1/2/3/4 years`, `5 years or more`) as `header_synonyms`: rejected —
  they head the *undiscounted* liquidity table (its own text: "comprising undiscounted cash flows …
  estimated interest payments"); teaching the schema that header would pull undiscounted columns in.
- **`without maturity` as an `ignore_header_synonyms` entry** (Avarda/Enity column): built, unit-tested
  against Avarda's real header — **provably inert** (`_bucket_synonym_hits` output identical with and
  without; no bucket synonym collides with the phrase), so not shipped per the no-undemonstrated-changes
  rule.

## 口径假设(已按真页核实) and the issue-#1 item-5 wording

The work order's hypothesis, verified against all six banks' real pages before writing:

**For banks: borrowings = issued debt securities + liabilities to credit institutions + subordinated
debt; deposits from the public are not borrowings — unless the report's own interest-bearing total
includes them, as Hoist Finance's does.**

Suggested wording for GitHub issue #1's assumption list (**supervisor to add**; this lane only writes
the text):

> 5. Bank borrowings (debt_maturity): a deposit-taking bank's "borrowings" is the report's own
>    interest-bearing total when one is printed — deposit-taking banks include customer deposits in it
>    (deposits from the public + issued debt securities + subordinated debt), so the deposits count.
>    When no such total row exists — true for 4 of the 6 Mid Cap bank reports we measured — customer
>    deposits are the bank's operating funding, not borrowings, and no single borrowed-money sub-row
>    may stand in for the total: the fields stay null. Banks' only maturity tables are the liquidity
>    note's undiscounted contractual cash flows and are never read. Verified on Hoist Finance, Norion
>    Bank, Morrow Bank, Avarda/TF Bank, Enity, Intrum (FY2025 reports), v090.

## Financials gap count — what needs the extract/parse layer (for supervisor dispatch)

1. **Zero/dash totals outside a stated sentence: 1** (Linc) — the model already answers 0 off the
   printed `Räntebärande skulder – –` row; `_value_in_quote` drops it ("printed on none of pages …
   computed"). Same family as v048's Creades item, one step further: the printed dash *is* the source,
   there is no digit to demand. General shape: a printed-nil total row (v078's `printed_nil`, today
   bucket-table-only) generalized to the total row.
2. **Two-column-layout scramble: 2** (Catella, Flerie) — the standing `parse.py` item (HANDOFF's own
   "Next"); both financials hide readable debt tables behind it, Flerie also loses `Konvertibellån
   104,8` to it.
3. **Locator blindness on non-standard debt vocabulary: 1** (Öresund) — zero candidates; its debt is
   hybrid bonds + lease liabilities. A `keywords` addition was *not* attempted (v048's Ovzon lesson:
   locator pulls drag parent/noise pages in without reaching the page either).
4. **Negative-signed liquidity rows read as totals: 1** (Catella pass2) — a quote-verified negative
   "total borrowings" survives every gate. A carrying total that is negative is a cash-flow outflow,
   not a carrying amount; the guard for this is prompt/extract territory.
5. **`_year_column` period misattribution: 1** (Tången's false "headed 2024" flag on a `31 december
   2025` table) — v041's MedCap family; does not change this section's values but corrupts any
   period-aware consumer.
