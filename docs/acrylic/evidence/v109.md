## Source 1 — year COLUMNS (first pass, `29f9f6e`)

`_fill_bucket_columns`' recorded pick, when its header was `_bucket_year_hits`' calendar-year fallback,
now also records the fallback's own column labels (`_bucket_header`'s `year_cols` → the pick's
`year_labels`); `_year_cols_read` re-reads them on the pick's own row. A dash in a year column is the
report's explicit 0 (v078's convention, one window finer); the years must close on the extraction's
`total_debt` within the check's own ±2. The three bucket fields and their summation of those same
year columns are v036/v095's, byte-identical.

Verified this round **on the full stored page text** (sidebar torn in, exactly as pymupdf printed it),
both with an all-null model answer and with the production shape (total quoted from the row): both give
`total_debt 2,332`, buckets 544/1,788/0 unchanged, and
`buckets_by_year = carrying [("2026",544),("2027",1394),("2028",144),("2029",250),("2030",0),("2031–",0)]`.
The permanent cases in `test_confidence.py` (first-pass block) cover the same page end-to-end plus the

## Source 2 — year ROWS, torn inline (this continuation)

The work order named BTS as the second real case: "日历年行 2026–2030, seed 11". BTS p.92's maturity
table prints one row per year — and pymupdf **tears it into a single line of `<year> <figure>` pairs**
(`data/kb/bts_2025` real text):

```
Liabilities to credit institutions
SEK thousands 12-31-25 12-31-24
Non-current liabilities 502,656 202,500
Current liabilities 77,141 218,453
Total 579,797 420,953
Maturity analyses for liabilities to credit institutions
SEK thousands 12-31-25 2026 77,141 2027 39 2028 300,039 2029 202,539 2030 39
Total 579,797
```

The first pass had declined all row-shaped tables ("every row-shaped year table in the corpus is torn…
no reader was invented for a shape nothing can exercise") — a verdict written against the pre-seed-11
corpus. With BTS in, exactly one clean, closing instance exists, and the torn inline form it arrives in
is deterministic:

- **`_inline_year_pairs`** parses a row from its own end: last token a figure (`_year_figure` — an
  `_AMOUNT` by `_row_amounts`' convention, or a lone dash = the report's explicit 0), the token before
  it its year, alternating left while each year is exactly the previous one minus 1; **≥2 pairs and the
  leftmost year = fiscal_year+1** (the `_bucket_year_hits` grid). Whatever sits left of the first year
  (`SEK thousands 12-31-25`) is furniture and stays unread. The strict −1 chain is the valve: a skipped
  year breaks it, and an unrelated year+number pair ("in 2026 3.00 per share") cannot chain.
- **Below the pairs**: at most one open-end tail row (`_YEAR_TAIL` on its own label — "Thereafter 260",
  "2031 and later 169" — exactly one figure), then the table's own **bare Total row printing exactly
  one figure** (`_after_label_figure`). BTS p.92's stacked two-column `Total 579,797 420,953` is the
  liabilities table's row and declines here.
- **Closures**: the years (tail included) must sum to the printed total AND to the extraction's
  `total_debt`, both within the check's own ±2; v103's `_table_scope` wrong-table guard runs on the
  total row; v101's liabilities-negative sign convention applies. Declines are silent — a metadata
  key, not a fill. The three bucket fields are untouched.
- **Scan set**: `_fill_bucket_columns` now records its own page walk (`scan_pages`, statement spread +
  identity-cited pages) into the pick before any valve — a page holding only a year-rows table records
  no pick of its own, and the row fallback still gets the exact pages the column read tried.

The **untorn one-row-per-year form is deliberately still not read**: nothing in the corpus exercises
it — BTS p.93's own NOTE 21 is that shape (`2027 18,415 / 2028 318,414 / 2029 211,452 / 2030 39 /
Total 548,320`) and declines anyway (non-current only: first year 2027 = fiscal_year+2, total a
narrower scope than `total_debt`) — no reader for a shape nothing can exercise, the file's own rule.
