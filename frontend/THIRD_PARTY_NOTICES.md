# Thinking Orbs

Ask uses `thinking-orbs` 0.3.1 from [Libraries.dev](https://libraries.dev/orbs).
Source: [Jakub Antalik's Libraries.dev](https://github.com/Jakubantalik/Libraries.dev/tree/main/packages/thinking-orbs).
Copyright (c) 2026 Jakub Antalik. MIT license.

The full license ships in `public/licenses/thinking-orbs.txt`. The library renders
locally with a 2D canvas, respects reduced motion and pauses when hidden or offscreen.
Our wrapper follows the app's theme and maps idle/pending to breathing/searching.
